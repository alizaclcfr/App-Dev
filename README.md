# My Favorite Movie: Howl's Moving Castle
**Howl's Moving Castle** is a 2004 Japanese animated fantasy film produced by **Studio Ghibli**.
Directed by *Hayao Miyazaki*, with music composed by *Joe Hisaishi*.
### Why I Like It
- **Love** – Learning to care for others.
- **Courage** – Finding strength despite difficulties.
- **Self-acceptance** – Recognizing your own worth.
> A beautiful story about love, courage, and finding a place where you belong.
